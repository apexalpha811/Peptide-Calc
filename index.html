Supplies Checklist

Tap each item to confirm you have it. Lay everything out on a clean, dry surface before starting.

  • Peptide vial — sealed, refrigerated (or per manufacturer), with intact rubber stopper
  • Bacteriostatic water (0.9% benzyl alcohol) — not sterile saline, not "sterile water for injection"
  • Drawing syringe + needle — typically 3 mL with 21-23 G, 1" needle (for drawing BAC water only)
  • Injection syringe — insulin syringe with fixed needle (29-31 G, 5/16" or 1/2")
  • Alcohol prep pads — sterile, individually wrapped (70% isopropyl)
  • Sharps container — FDA-cleared, puncture-resistant
  • Clean workspace — wiped surface, hands washed with soap for 20 sec
  • Cotton ball / gauze — for post-injection pressure
Bacteriostatic water vs. sterile water: BAC water contains 0.9% benzyl alcohol, which inhibits microbial growth and lets you use the vial multiple times. Sterile water has no preservative — only safe for single-use reconstitution.

Procedure

  1. Wipe the BAC water vial stopper with an alcohol pad. Let it air-dry (don't blow on it).
  2. Pull back on the drawing syringe plunger to pre-load 2.0 mL of air.
  3. Insert the needle straight into the BAC water vial stopper. Push the air in (this equalizes pressure).
  4. Invert the vial. Slowly pull back to 2.0 mL of BAC water.
  5. Tap the syringe to dislodge bubbles. Push them out and re-draw if needed.
  6. Remove the needle straight out — keep it sterile.

Reconstitute the Peptide

Inject the BAC water into the peptide vial — slowly, and against the glass wall.

Ready

Critical technique: angle the needle

  1. Wipe the peptide vial stopper with alcohol. Let dry.
  2. Insert the needle and tilt the syringe so the BAC water runs down the inner glass wall — never directly onto the powder.
  3. Push the plunger slowly (over 5-10 seconds). Direct streams can shear the peptide.
  4. Let the vacuum equalize. Don't withdraw plunger.
  5. Withdraw the needle. Discard into sharps container.
Why "slowly + against the wall"? Many peptides are fragile peptide chains. Forceful streams or direct impact on the lyophilized cake can denature the molecule and reduce potency.

Dissolve, Don't Shake

Let the peptide dissolve gently. Then inspect for clarity.

Ready

Mixing technique

  1. Set the vial upright. Wait 30-60 seconds — most peptides dissolve on contact.
  2. If undissolved powder remains, gently roll or swirl the vial between your palms.
  3. Never shake. Shaking creates foam and shears peptide bonds.
  4. Inspect: solution should be clear and colorless. No haze, particles, or floaters.
  5. Label the vial: peptide name, concentration (mg/mL), reconstitution date.
  6. Store refrigerated (typically 36-46°F / 2-8°C) unless manufacturer specifies otherwise.
Reject the vial if: cloudy, discolored, has floating particles, or has been frozen. Once reconstituted, most peptides are stable 28-30 days refrigerated — confirm with manufacturer.

SubQ injection — step by step

  1. Site rotation: abdomen (2 in. from navel), outer thigh, or back of arm. Rotate sites to avoid lipohypertrophy.
  2. Wipe site with alcohol pad. Let dry completely (wet alcohol stings).
  3. Draw your dose — pull the calculated insulin units of reconstituted peptide.
  4. Tap out air bubbles; gently push to expel any air.
  5. Pinch a fold of skin to lift fat away from muscle.
  6. Insert at 45-90° in one steady motion (90° if you have enough fat; 45° if very lean).
  7. Press plunger slowly and steadily — count 5 seconds.
  8. Hold needle in place 5 seconds, then withdraw at the same angle.
  9. Apply gentle pressure with cotton — do not rub.
  10. Drop syringe directly into sharps container. Never recap.
  • Confirmed correct peptide and concentration on label
  • Drew correct number of insulin units (no air at tip)
  • Cleaned and dried injection site
  • Rotated from previous injection site (≥1 inch away)
  • Disposed of needle in sharps container, not trash

Safety Checks & Common Errors

Most mistakes happen in math or technique. Review before every injection.

⚠ Confusing mg with mcg

1 mg = 1000 mcg. A "250 mcg dose" is 0.25 mg, not 250 mg.

✓ Always convert to the same unit before dividing.

⚠ Confusing mL with insulin units

On a 100-unit insulin syringe, 100 IU = 1 mL. So 0.1 mL = 10 IU, not 100.

✓ Multiply mL × 100 to get insulin units (for U-100 syringes).

⚠ Using sterile water instead of BAC water

Sterile water lacks preservative. Multi-use vials reconstituted with it can grow bacteria.

✓ Use bacteriostatic water for any vial used over multiple days.

⚠ Shaking the vial

Vigorous shaking foams the solution and shears peptide bonds, reducing potency.

✓ Swirl gently or roll between palms only.

⚠ Injecting BAC water directly onto powder

A direct stream can denature delicate peptides.

✓ Aim down the inner glass wall, push slowly.

⚠ Skipping site rotation

Repeat injections in the same spot cause lipohypertrophy (lumps) and erratic absorption.

✓ Rotate at least 1 inch each injection.

⚠ Injecting wet alcohol

Stings, increases tissue irritation, and may inactivate some peptides at the site.

✓ Let the prep pad fully air-dry first.

⚠ Recapping needles

Leading cause of accidental needle-stick injuries.

✓ Drop directly into sharps container, no recap.

⚠ Storing reconstituted vials at room temperature

Most reconstituted peptides degrade above ~46°F (8°C).

✓ Refrigerate; do not freeze unless specified.

⚠ Using a vial past stability window

Reconstituted peptides typically last 28-30 days refrigerated.

✓ Label with reconstitution date; discard at expiry.

⚠ Hitting muscle on subQ injection

Wrong absorption profile, more painful, possible bruising.

✓ Pinch fat fold; use 45° if lean. Use 5/16" or 1/2" needles.

⚠ Drawing through a coring stopper

Repeatedly punching the same spot can dislodge rubber bits into the solution.

✓ Insert at slight angle, vary entry point on the stopper.

When to stop and contact a clinician: persistent injection-site reaction, allergic symptoms (hives, swelling, trouble breathing), persistent or unusual side effects, or any sign of vial contamination.
